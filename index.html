Equation used

For the simplified static-thrust model:

T = Ct × ρ × n² × D⁴

Rearranging:

n = √[T / (Ct × ρ × D⁴)]

RPM = 60 × n

T = thrust in newtons; Ct = dimensionless thrust coefficient; ρ = air density in kg/m³; n = revolutions per second; D = propeller diameter in metres.

Ct depends on the actual propeller and operating conditions. Unless Ct is measured or supplied for your exact propeller, the result is only a mathematical estimate. Do not use the result alone to select a motor, battery or safe operating speed.

Storage & limitations

This version stores inventory in the browser's local storage. It does not automatically synchronize data between different phones or users. Export a backup before clearing browser data.

Product URLs and specification sources are stored as references. This application does not automatically open product pages, scrape specifications or independently verify manufacturer claims.

Engineering constants

Standard gravitational acceleration used: 9.80665 m/s²

Mass conversion: 1 kg = 1000 g

Thrust and weight are expressed in newtons (N).

Keep units consistent. A mass in kilograms is not the same quantity as a weight in newtons.
